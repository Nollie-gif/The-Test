# OBS-006 — In-Game Time Continuity Lapse

## Evidence class

Real-play observation.

## Source system

Mission 10 live gameplay.

## Observation

During Day 19 play, the user had to remind the DM-agent that the in-game time reference had been forgotten or omitted after the scene had progressed.

The campaign clock/time state existed as an operational concern, but maintaining the temporal reference still consumed agent attention during narration.

## Why this matters to The-Test

This is evidence for the question of whether established time should be delivered as compact persistent scene context rather than repeatedly reconstructed or remembered conversationally.

## Candidate interface lesson

A scene/context packet may need to surface current day/time as a high-salience field and refresh it only at meaningful boundaries.
