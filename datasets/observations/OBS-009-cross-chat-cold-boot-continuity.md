# OBS-009 — Cross-chat cold boot reconstructed the research frontier

## Observation
A fresh project chat was given only a compact boot prompt and instructed to reconstruct The-Test from durable repository state rather than predecessor-chat memory.

The fresh agent correctly recovered the core research purpose, the `PRO / RSH / EXP / OBS / PRT / RUN / RES` evidence architecture, all three active research tracks, the exact purpose of `EXP-001`, the registered Mission 10 observations, the Control Server/context-packet research directions, the implemented validation infrastructure, and the immediate frontier: design and execution of `RUN-001`.

It also independently identified two small repository gaps: the run artifact/schema did not explicitly require a wrong-route/wrong-target metric or failure stage despite the research protocol naming them, and the `RES-###` family existed conceptually without a template or validator coverage.

## Why this matters
This is positive real-world evidence for the idea that durable, structured, targeted repository context can preserve cross-session continuity without replaying a giant conversation transcript.

The observation is stronger than a simple summary-recall success because the fresh agent also detected small internal inconsistencies while preserving the project's stated research boundaries.

## What this does not prove
This single cold boot does not establish causality, generalize to other agents/models, or prove that the current handoff architecture is optimal. It is an observation suitable for later controlled testing under RSH-002.
