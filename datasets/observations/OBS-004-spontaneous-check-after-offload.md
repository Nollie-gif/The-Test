# OBS-004 — Spontaneous Deception Check After Offload

## Evidence class

Real-play observation.

## Source system

Mission 10 live scene play.

## Observation

During a scene with Victor, the DM-agent independently called for a Deception check after Bor intentionally planted false information as a trap.

The user identified this as a notable first in Mission 10: after a long period in which the user usually initiated checks, the DM-agent recognized the meaningful uncertainty and requested the roll on its own.

This occurred after substantial temporal, runtime, and routing knowledge had been moved out of conversational memory into structured external systems.

## Why this matters to The-Test

This does **not** prove causation.

It is, however, a useful qualitative signal for the central research hypothesis: when operational bookkeeping consumes less cognitive attention, the agent may have more capacity to notice semantic/gameplay opportunities autonomously.

## Candidate experiment

Compare identical or structurally similar scenes under different context/routing loads and measure:

- whether the agent recognizes when a check is warranted;
- whether the chosen check is appropriate;
- whether the agent initiates the mechanic without prompting;
- whether unnecessary checks increase as a side effect.
