# RSH-003 — Backstage / Knowledge Boundary Integrity

## Research question

Which agent-facing context structure best preserves who knows what, who may reveal it, and which information belongs only to the hidden/system layer?

## Scope

Study provenance, NPC knowledge ownership, reveal authority, hidden mechanics, player-facing leads, and whether structured context packets reduce boundary leaks without bloating context.

## Primary signals

- wrong knowledge owner;
- unauthorized/incorrect reveal route;
- hidden-mechanic or dice/meta leakage;
- human correction;
- successful targeted retrieval;
- context size needed to preserve provenance.
