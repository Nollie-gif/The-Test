# RSH-002 — Context / Temporal Load Offload

## Research question

Does reducing temporal, runtime, and routing burden improve an AI agent's ability to maintain continuity and notice semantically important opportunities during live interaction?

## Scope

Study scene-time continuity, compact runtime snapshots, lookup pressure, context size, and whether offloading operational memory improves semantic initiative without increasing hallucination or over-triggering mechanics.

## Primary signals

- missed or incorrect time references;
- repeated/broad lookups;
- context volume required;
- unnecessary tool churn;
- spontaneous correct mechanic recognition;
- inappropriate/unnecessary mechanic calls;
- human correction/intervention.
